# Festival Entry Questions

Source check date: 2026-05-20

FilmFreeway is useful because it centralises discovery and submission, but it can also make it too easy to pay for weak-fit entries. Use this page as a pause before checkout.

## Fit Questions

- Is this a physical festival, an online festival, an awards event, a contest, or something else?
- Does the film fit the runtime, category, completion date and premiere rules?
- Is the festival actually interested in documentary work like this?
- Is there a real audience, venue, screening, Q&A, industry path, community fit or learning value?
- Would this still be worth entering if no laurel arrived?

## Cost Questions

- What is the entry fee, currency and deadline tier?
- Is there an earlybird deadline, waiver, discount or student/local option?
- What is the total spend if we submit to several categories?
- Is the fee worth it compared with travel, captions, DCP, poster, stills, local screening or marketing costs?
- Is the refund/cancellation wording clear enough?

## Readiness Questions

- Is the film actually festival-ready, or is it still a rough cut needing feedback?
- Are screener, trailer, poster, stills, synopsis, credits, director bio and press kit ready?
- Are captions, subtitles and accessibility notes ready?
- Are music, archive, people, place, cultural and location permissions clear enough?
- Could public submission or selection create privacy, safety, cultural or relationship problems?

## Red Flag Questions

- Does the festival show a clear venue, location, team and contact pathway?
- Are past winners, screenings, photos, videos or filmmaker reviews visible?
- Are there lots of paid award categories without clear screening value?
- Are the promises too big for the evidence shown?
- Do public reviews or forums show repeated complaints?
- Is the social media presence real, active and connected to the festival?
