# Public / Private Boundaries

The builders are allowed to create working notes quickly, but publishing still needs human judgement.

## Usually Public-Safe After Review

- Film title, year, director and public synopsis.
- Public screening date, venue and host details.
- Reviewed source links and citations.
- General discussion questions.
- Public follow-up actions such as reading lists, workshop prompts or article outlines.

## Keep Private Or Review Carefully

- Private contact details.
- Raw local file paths.
- Unpublished interview notes.
- Sensitive personal stories.
- Protected places, cultural permissions, safety notes or venue access details.
- Claims that have not been checked against a source.
- Notes about who is missing or misrepresented when the wording is still rough.

## Draft Label

Generated files should stay marked as draft until a human deliberately moves them to a reviewed state.
