This page is the public-facing version of the workflow. The Markdown file in docs/ is a source note for repo maintenance and agent handoffs.

Full flow

Start with the film frame. End with a clean handoff.

Jump straight to a builder when you already know the job, or follow the stages when the project still needs shape.

01 / FoundationFilm Profile

Name the film, claim, makers, represented people, gaps and first source leads.

 02 / FoundationTheme and Motif

Notice repeated ideas and images before the research map spreads too wide.

 03 / Research mapResearch Angle

Choose the question that the next source trail needs to test.

 04 / Research mapSource Trail

Record what each source proves, what it does not prove, and when it was checked.

 05 / Research mapCharacter / Subject

Track representation, agency, missing context and dignity risks.

 06 / Research mapInterview Questions

Prepare the right questions for filmmakers, subjects, critics, local experts or audiences.

 07 / AssemblyScene Analysis

Separate evidence, implication, film language and uncertainty scene by scene.

 08 / AssemblyRun Sheet

Compile scenes into a working film order with evidence beats and edit decisions.

 09 / FestivalFestival Entry

Compare FilmFreeway-style options by fit, cost, rules, materials and red flags.

 10 / ViewingViewing Notes

Capture notes once there is a watchable cut, screening copy or completed release.

 11 / Public contextEvent Notes

Record venue, audience, questions, responses, permissions and practical follow-up.

 12 / Follow-upFollow-Up

Shape public-safe actions, article ideas, workshops, research trails and private tasks.

 13 / HandoffAgent Handoff

Pass one bounded job to a future agent with sources, boundaries and acceptance checks.
